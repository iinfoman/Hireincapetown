# Call list — unverified candidates

**Nothing in this file may go on the site.** These names came from web search
snippets on 19 September 2026. Not one has been phoned, no registration number
has been checked, and no document has been seen. Treat every line as a lead to
follow, not a fact.

Work down the list with [VETTING.md](VETTING.md) open. A call that goes well
becomes a listing the same day.

## First, a filter that saves you time

Search engines surface whoever is best at SEO. In South African trades that is
disproportionately **lead-generation companies** — they take your call and sell
it on to whichever contractor is free. They are the thing this site exists to
beat, so recognising them matters more than any individual name below.

Tells, in order of reliability:

- **One page per suburb.** `/moving-companies/rondebosch/`,
  `/tokai-movers/`, `/cape-town-southern-suburbs/`. A real business has one
  contact page, not forty.
- **An 087 number.** Non-geographic VoIP, usually a call centre. A **021**
  landline is a real Cape Town premises and a much better sign.
- **No named human anywhere.** No owner, no team, no photographs of actual vans.
- **"We service all of South Africa."** Then they are not a Southern Suburbs
  plumber.

On the phone the question that settles it: *"Do you do the work yourself, or do
you pass it to someone?"* Lead-gen operations answer that honestly, because
they think it is a selling point.

## Better sources than this file

Everything above is second-hand. These are first-hand and will produce better
listings faster:

1. **Suburb Facebook groups.** Search "plumber" inside the group. Names that
   recur with real thanks underneath have been vetted by neighbours.
2. **Referrals from the first business you list.** Ask who they call when
   they are booked out. Tradespeople are blunt about who is competent.
3. **Van signage.** A sign-written van with an 021 number is someone with
   premises and something to lose.

## What "running with real businesses" actually takes

Six to ten calls. Each one is about fifteen minutes. Three or four will go
well. Those become listings the same afternoon through
[the form](https://hireincapetown.co.za/tools/new-listing/), and the site is
live with real, checked businesses by this evening.

There is no version of this where the checking is skipped. The checking is
the product.
